Terms of service

Your account and your studio

You sign in with your Google account. You must be at least 13 years old, and at least 18 (or have a parent or guardian's permission) to pay for anything. You get your own studio: its games, data and people are yours, and other customers can't see them. Anything done in your studio by you or the people you invite counts as done by you, so invite only people you trust.

Hawkes Studio is its own product. Your account here is separate from your accounts on hawkesgen.ai and Hawkes Gen, even though the same Google account signs in to all of them, and credits don't move between them.

Plans, credits and payment

  • The free plan covers one game with playtests and reviews. Paid plans add more games, nightly runs, desktop builds, agent fixes and the PM; your plan's page in Settings says what yours includes.
  • Paid plans renew every month until you cancel. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep the plan until the end of the month you paid for.
  • Each paid month adds credits to your studio (one credit is worth one US cent). Credits have no cash value and can't be moved to another account.
  • Payments go through Stripe, in US dollars; your bank may add its own fees.
  • We may change prices. A change never touches what you already paid for, and a plan's new price starts at a renewal, after we tell you.

Your own AI accounts

Talk and the agents in your studio run on an AI account that is yours, never ours: Claude (your Anthropic API key or your Claude subscription), Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Gemini or Antigravity. You connect it in your studio, and you can remove it any time. Your use of that account is between you and the provider: they bill you, and their terms apply. We store what you give us encrypted, use it only for your own studio's work, and never show it back except its last few characters.

Your games, repositories and builds

Your games are yours. We use the addresses, repositories and builds you give us only to build, play and review your games for you, and, when you ask for it, to have agents change them. Playtests run your game in a real browser on cloud servers with graphics cards. We don't use your games, code, prompts or results to train models, and we don't show them to other customers.

Agents change your code only on their own branches and through pull requests; nothing goes live without you. Review what an agent changed before you ship it: it's your game and your call.

Quiet studios

  • A studio nobody has signed in to or run anything in for 14 days goes to sleep to save resources. Your data stays; the next time you open it, it wakes up in a few seconds.
  • A studio on the free plan that nobody has signed in to for 90 days is deleted with its data. We email you first and wait 14 days; signing in once keeps it. A studio on a paid plan is never deleted for being quiet.

What you can't do

  • Add games or material you don't have the rights to, or anything illegal.
  • Use the service to attack, overload or scrape other sites, or point it at sites you don't control.
  • Get around your plan's limits or billing, reverse-engineer the service, or resell access without our written permission.

We may stop work or suspend a studio that breaks these rules. If we close your studio for any other reason, we'll refund the paid time and credits you haven't used.

The service

We work hard to keep Hawkes Studio running, but the service is provided "as is". Reviews and agents are AI: they can miss things or get them wrong, and we can't promise a particular result or uptime. We may change, add or remove features.

To the extent the law allows, we aren't liable for indirect or consequential losses, and our total liability to you for anything to do with the service is limited to what you paid us in the 12 months before the claim.

Ending

You can stop using Hawkes Studio and ask us to delete your studio at any time. We may change these terms; if a change matters, we'll tell you in your studio or by email before it takes effect. Using the service after that means you accept the new terms.

Privacy policy

What we collect

  • Account: from Google, your email address, your name and Google's id for your account. We never see your Google password.
  • Your studio: your games' addresses and repositories, what you tell Talk, the roadmap and backlog, the playtests' videos, screenshots and reviews, and the people you invite.
  • Billing: your plan, what you paid and your credits. Card details go straight to Stripe; we never see or store them.
  • Your AI accounts: the keys or sign-ins you connect, stored encrypted.
  • Usage: sign-in times and basic request logs (IP address, browser), kept for security and troubleshooting.

Cookies

We set a cookie to keep you signed in. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

To run your studio, play and review your games, take payments, prevent abuse, answer you when you write to us, and tell you about changes to the service. We don't sell your data and we don't use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

These companies run parts of the service. Each handles your data only to provide that part:

  • Google Cloud: the servers and storage your studio runs on (United States).
  • Cloudflare: the network in front of the service.
  • RunPod and Modal: the GPU servers your playtests run on (United States).
  • Anthropic, and the other AI providers you connect (Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Google Gemini and Antigravity): Talk, the agents and the reviews, on your own account.
  • Stripe: payments.
  • GitHub: your game's repository, when you connect one.
  • Google: sign-in.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect people from harm.

How long we keep it

We keep your studio's data while it exists (see "Quiet studios" above). If you ask us to delete your studio, we delete its data within 30 days. We keep billing records as long as tax and accounting law require.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your studio at any time by writing to us. Where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California) may give you further rights, and we honor them.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Every studio runs separately with its own data, and its keys and your AI accounts' credentials are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we'll tell you.

Refunds

  • Plans: cancel any time to stop the next renewal. Within 14 days of a monthly charge, we refund it in full if you haven't used any of that month's credits.
  • Charged in error, or twice: tell us and we'll refund it.
  • Something didn't work: if a playtest or a feature you paid for clearly didn't do what it says, tell us and we'll make it right, usually with credits.

Card refunds go back through Stripe and usually reach your account in 5 to 10 business days. Write to us from the email on your account.

Contact

Questions, requests about your data, or a refund: support@hawkesgen.ai.
