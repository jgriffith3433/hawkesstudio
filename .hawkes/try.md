1. Open the Studio dashboard on beta and go to the Roadmap tab.
2. See a card per milestone: "N of M items done" and a progress bar.
3. Under each card, every item shows its state and a sentence saying what blocks it (or that nothing does).
4. Leave the page open and change a task elsewhere (e.g. approve something in the Inbox): the card updates without a reload.
5. Switch the view selector to "Items only" to get the old list.
