HAWKES GEN

Generative AI and an automated studio, behind every Hawkes game and site.

Two services run the R&D floor of Hawkes Biomedical: one makes the art, video, 3D, music and voices; the other builds, plays and reviews every product every day.

Hawkes Gen

The shared generative-AI service. Every game, site and agent asks it for assets, so no project deals with GPU servers, models or API keys.

What it does today

  • Images: text-to-image, seamless textures, editing, paint-over from sketches or grey-box renders, relighting, upscaling, 360° skies
  • Characters: trains a character model on its poses, then draws it on-model in any pose or scene
  • Video: image-to-video, text-to-video, motion transfer, talking and singing characters, frame interpolation
  • 3D: meshes from one image or several views, with PBR textures
  • Music, voice and sound effects: songs and loops, voice lines, designed voices, SFX
  • Writing and vision: text, JSON designs and art review
  • Pipelines: chains of tools as one recipe, best-of-N takes picked against the art direction
  • A GPU fleet started on demand and shared through one queue, a library of everything made with its recipe, and usage and cost per project

Hawkes Studio

An automated game studio. Every day it builds, playtests and reviews every product, and agents fix what it finds.

What it does today

  • The daily loop: build, playtest in a real browser, review the footage, and a morning report
  • Playtests: each game's own bot plays through real input, on desktop and phone, with video, screenshots and frame rates
  • Reviews: findings filed into one backlog, merged across days, with regressions called out
  • Agents: fix bugs and build roadmap tasks, each as its own pull request
  • A beta lane: every change lands on a beta site first, and going live is a person's call, with rollback
  • Talk: plan the roadmap by chat or by voice, with a producer that keeps the work moving
